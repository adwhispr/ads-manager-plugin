# AdWhispr Ads Manager

Launch, manage and optimize your own ad accounts from chat, on seven platforms: Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google Ads, TikTok, X, ChatGPT Ads, Reddit and LinkedIn.

Install the plugin, sign in to AdWhispr once, connect an ad account on the platform's own sign-in screen, and ask:

- "What's wasting money in my ad account?"
- "Pause everything that has not converted this month."
- "Launch a Google Search campaign for my store at 20 a day."
- "Give me a Monday report every week."

## What it does

- **Reads** real spend, clicks and conversions on every connected account, with keyword-level diagnostics on Google Search.
- **Changes** budgets, names, geography, keywords and negative keywords, pause and resume, each with a preview you confirm first.
- **Launches** complete campaigns from your own creatives: Google Search (with call buttons), YouTube, Shopping, Meta image, carousel and video ads, TikTok video ads, promoted posts on X and Reddit, chat cards on ChatGPT, Sponsored Content on LinkedIn.
- **Adds** Meta audiences (website visitors, lookalikes) and Google or ChatGPT ad groups to existing campaigns.

Every campaign is created paused. Nothing spends until you resume it.

## Skills

- **Manage and optimize**: `ads-manager`, `wasted-spend-audit`, `budget-reallocation`, `weekly-ad-review`, `agency-multi-account`.
- **Launch, per platform**: `google-ads`, `meta-ads`, `tiktok-ads`, `x-ads`, `chatgpt-ads`, `reddit-ads`, `linkedin-ads`, and `local-business-ads` for shops and services.
- **`get-started`** and **`connect-ad-account`**: the two-minute first run and the one-click account connection.

## Safety

- Ad accounts connect through each platform's own consent screen. AdWhispr never sees your sign-in and never asks for API keys in chat.
- Every launch, budget change, targeting change and resume shows a preview first and applies only after you confirm.
- Numbers come from your ad accounts. The plugin never invents metrics.
- Data flows only to AdWhispr's own API. No hooks, no local code.
